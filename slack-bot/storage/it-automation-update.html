Employee Self-Service Password Reset

Any team member can change their own GSD account password directly from Slack — no IT involvement needed.

/gsdaccount available to all employees
1
Employee types /gsdaccount in any Slack channel or DM.
2
A form opens showing their corporate account (name@gsdoutsources.com), detected automatically.
3
They enter and confirm a new password — validated for strength in real time.
4
Password updates immediately across Gmail, Drive, and all GSD tools. Confirmation sent via DM.
✓ No IT ticket required Google Workspace Self-service
🛡️

IT-Initiated Remote Password Reset

IT can send a secure password-reset request to any employee directly from Slack — the employee sets their own new password.

/resetpassword @username or email — IT team only
1
IT types /resetpassword @employee or /resetpassword email@gsd…
2
The employee receives a direct message with a "Set New Password" button — IT never sees the new password.
3
Employee clicks the button, a secure form opens, they choose their new password.
4
Password updated. IT receives a confirmation DM that the reset was completed.
✓ IT notified on completion Google Workspace IT access only
