🔐 IT Self-Service

Change Your GSD Password
Directly from Slack

No portal. No ticket. Just type a command and your password updates in seconds.

1
Type the command in Slack
Open any channel or a direct message and type:
/gsdaccount then press Enter
2
Fill in the password form
A window will open automatically showing your account:
Change Password
✕
🔑  Your account: firstname.lastname@gsdoutsources.com
Enter new password
Re-enter new password
Min 8 characters — uppercase, lowercase and at least one number.
Cancel
Update Password
3
Your password must meet these requirements
✓
At least 8 characters
✓
At least 1 uppercase (A, B…)
✓
At least 1 lowercase (a, b…)
✓
At least 1 number (1, 2, 3…)
4
Click "Update Password" — done!
The bot will send you a confirmation in a direct message:
✅ Password updated successfully!
Account: firstname.lastname@gsdoutsources.com
The change is effective immediately across all your GSD devices.

⚠️ Important Notes

👁️ The text you type is visible while typing. Use this command from your own device, never on a shared or public screen.
🔒 You can only change your own password. The bot detects your account automatically from your Slack profile.
🚨 If you receive a confirmation message without having requested it, contact IT immediately — someone may have accessed your Slack.
🕐 The new password applies to Google Workspace, Gmail, Drive and all GSD tools linked to your corporate account.
